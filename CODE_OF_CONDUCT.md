# Code of conduct

Cablebag is a small project with a small community: issues, pull requests and discussions on this
repository. The rule is short. Be decent to each other.

In practice that means: assume the other person means well, stay on the subject, and disagree with ideas
rather than with people. Nobody here owes anyone their time; a bug report that goes unanswered for a while,
or a request that is turned down, is not an insult. Likewise, a maintainer who says no should say why.

Not welcome, in any form: insults, harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised language, publishing
someone's private information, and deliberately wasting people's time. This applies to everyone, the
maintainer included, and everywhere the project is discussed under its name.

If something goes wrong, you can raise it in two ways. For anything sensitive, or anything about a specific
person, use the private channel in the [Security tab](https://github.com/maferick/cablebag/security)
(**Report a vulnerability**; it is the same private form, and conduct reports are read there too). For
anything that can be discussed in the open, open an issue. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept private
where asked, and acted on as soon as possible.

What happens depends on what happened: a quiet word, a removed comment, or, for repeated or serious
behaviour, being blocked from the repository.
